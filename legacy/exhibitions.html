Archive

Exhibitions

A chronological archive of solo and group exhibitions featuring work by Shivjeet Potdar and Conscious Omnium.

2026

Liminal Landscapes

Gallery Collective — Mumbai, India

Solo Exhibition · March – April 2026

A solo presentation of twelve new works exploring transitional spaces and the dissolution of perceived boundaries. The exhibition transforms the gallery into an immersive environment where paintings occupy architectural thresholds between light and shadow.

12 works · Oil, mixed media, pigment on canvas

Emerging Frequencies

Art Forward — Pune, India

Group Exhibition · January 2026

A curated group show featuring five contemporary artists investigating material processes and their relationship to consciousness. Potdar presented three large-scale works from the Embodied Cartography series alongside sculptural and textile-based practices.

3 works · Mixed media on canvas

2025

The Weight of Looking

Emerging Visions — New Delhi, India

Solo Exhibition · September – October 2025

A contemplative solo exhibition of eight works examining the relationship between sustained attention and visual experience. Each painting was displayed with generous spacing to invite prolonged looking, transforming the gallery into a space of meditative encounter.

8 works · Oil and mixed media on canvas and linen

Material Dialogues

Studio Collective — Mumbai, India

Group Exhibition · April 2025

An interdisciplinary group show exploring materiality across painting, sculpture, and textile. Potdar contributed two mixed-media paintings from early studies in the Strata series, marking his first public exhibition of the work that would evolve into the Embodied Cartography collection.

2 works · Mixed media on canvas

2024

First Surfaces

Open Studio — Mumbai, India

Open Studio · December 2024

An intimate open studio event presenting the foundational works of the Conscious Omnium practice. Five paintings and a series of studies offered visitors a first glimpse into Potdar's developing visual language.

5 paintings + studies · Oil and pigment on canvas
