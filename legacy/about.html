About the Artist

Shivjeet Potdar

Artist · Founder of Conscious Omnium

Shivjeet Potdar is a contemporary artist whose practice moves between painting, mixed media, and installation. Working from his studio, he develops bodies of work that interrogate the nature of perception and the residues of lived experience.

His work is characterised by a material intelligence — an intuitive layering of oil, pigment, gold leaf, and found elements that construct surfaces of geological complexity. Each painting becomes a site of accumulation and erasure, presence and absence.

Through Conscious Omnium, Potdar positions art as a practice of deep attention — a slow, deliberate engagement with the textures of consciousness itself.

Artist Statement

"I work to create surfaces that hold time — where gesture, material, and memory accumulate into something that resists easy reading. The paintings ask for slow looking. They want to be lived with."

— Shivjeet Potdar
