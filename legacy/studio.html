Shivjeet Potdar

The Work in Becoming

Studio & Process

The studio is where possibility remains unresolved — where materials speak before intention shapes them. Every painting begins here.

Process

"The process begins with material. Raw pigment ground by hand. Oil paint mixed to precise viscosity. Canvas stretched and primed with layers of gesso that become part of the final surface."

Every mark carries the weight of the gesture that made it. There is no separation between thinking and making — the studio practice is one of continuous material dialogue. Paint is built up and scraped back. Surfaces are layered, sanded, re-worked.

The paintings that emerge from this process carry the memory of every state they've passed through. What appears as a single image is actually an archaeology of decisions, accidents, and recoveries.

Materials

Oil, Pigment, Gold Leaf, Canvas, Linen, Found Objects

Each material carries its own history and behaviour. The studio practice involves learning to listen to what materials want to do, then directing that energy towards an image that feels inevitable rather than imposed.

"A painting is finished when it has its own presence — when it no longer needs me."

Shivjeet Potdar
