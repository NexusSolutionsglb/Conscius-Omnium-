Artist Statement

"Art as an inquiry into perception, presence, memory and the spaces between what is seen and felt."

Conscious Omnium is a contemporary art practice founded by Shivjeet Potdar — an ongoing investigation into the liminal territories of human experience. Working across oil, mixed media, and pigment, each body of work seeks to dissolve the boundaries between interior states and external landscapes.
